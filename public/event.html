Questions

The things we get asked.

What kind of content is allowed?

Sssion Studio supports the full spectrum of sensual movement — from gentle somatic flow to advanced pole, burlesque-inspired choreography, and floor work. We use a three-tier "spice rating" system (General / Mature / Explicit) so creators teach in their natural voice and students opt into what they're ready for. Mature and Explicit content requires age confirmation. Full nudity and content designed for sexual gratification fall outside our scope — but we've worked hard to draw lines that protect creators rather than restrict them.

How does payment work?

We use Stripe Connect, which means creators get paid directly to their own bank accounts, not to us. The platform takes a transparent percentage fee — no hidden cuts, no held funds, no surprise deductions. Founding creators lock in lower fees for life.

Can my account get shut down without warning?

No. Our content policy is published, specific, and built in collaboration with the community. If something we publish is unclear, we work it out with you — we don't ban-first-ask-questions-later. Account terminations require documented policy violations and we provide notice and appeal paths in nearly every case. We're explicitly building the opposite of what creators have experienced on mainstream platforms.

I already have a Patreon / Ko-fi / OnlyFans. Why switch?

You don't have to switch — many of our creators run multiple platforms. But Sssion Studio is built specifically for movement instruction in a way general platforms aren't: structured class libraries, video-first storefronts, student progression tracking, a community built around the work. Patreon is a tip jar; we're a studio.

Do I need a big audience to apply for the founding cohort?

No. We care about the work, not follower counts. We're looking for instructors who teach with intention, treat their students well, and are ready to build something that lasts. If that's you, apply.

When does the platform launch?

Founding creators are being onboarded now, with student doors opening shortly after. Joining the waitlist is the only way to get an early invitation — and once we hit our founding cohort cap, the waitlist becomes the line for the next opening.
