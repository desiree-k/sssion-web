The hour in your studio is the part people remember. The six days in between are where they drift.

## The part of your studio nobody can book

Most students don't leave because of a class. They leave because life gets loud and nothing pulls them back between visits.

The friendships, the encouragement, the "wait, show me how you did that" all happen in the room. Then the door closes, and it stops.

That connection is why people come in the first place. In ABC Fitness's Fall 2025 Wellness Watch, 57% of active consumers said social interaction and connection are the main reasons they join a fitness community ([source](https://athletechnews.com/people-are-joining-gyms-for-social-connection-abc-fitness-data/)).

## A home for your studio's community

Sssion gives your studio a Space of its own: your members, your instructors and your sessions, in one place you control.

- **Progress Shares**: a video feed where members share what they're working on, and cheer each other on.
- **Rooms**: one for each class, level or program. Some are for video, some for conversation.
- **Your instructors**: they post and look after the rooms you give them.
- **Sessions to revisit**: classes, breakdowns and recordings members can come back to.
- **Live sessions**: two-way video, so class can feel like class, even from home.

Your community is members-only. Only people you let in can join the conversation, and a room can be kept for a specific group, like your teacher-training cohort.
