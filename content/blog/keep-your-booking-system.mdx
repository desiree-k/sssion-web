Sssion doesn't replace your booking or billing. It adds the part those tools were never built for: a place for your members to belong between classes.

## What stays where it is

Your schedule, class packs, memberships, payments and check-in all stay in the system you already use. Your front desk doesn't learn anything new, and your members keep booking the way they do now.

## What Sssion adds

A private home for your studio's community: rooms for each class or program, a feed where members share their progress, sessions they can revisit, and live sessions for the days they can't make it in. Your instructors post and look after the rooms you give them.

## Nothing to connect

There's no integration to set up. Sssion doesn't plug into your booking system, and it doesn't need your member list. You decide who comes in.

## Three ways to let your members in

### Members only, you approve

**Best for:** Most studios, to start

Your community is free to join and every request comes to you. Check it against your roster and approve.

### Open community

**Best for:** Studios using community to meet new students

Anyone who finds your Space can join straight away. Keep some rooms for members only.

### Paid access through your own checkout

**Best for:** Add-on programs, like an on-demand library or teacher training

Add your purchase page as the offering's payment link. Members buy there, request access in Sssion, and you approve once you've confirmed.

## How long access lasts

When you set up an offering, you choose how long access lasts: forever, or a set number of days.

Sssion doesn't see renewals in your booking system. For most studios, the simplest setup is ongoing access to the community, with set-length access saved for programs that have a clear end, like a six-week course.
